# 1.0.0

First release: all of 1.15 Buzzy Bees on 1.12.2. Needs Backport Core 1.1.0 or newer, which needs Fluidlogged API 3.3.3 or newer.
Everything is in vanilla's creative tabs where 1.19.2 lists it (bee nests and beehives with the decoration blocks, the honey block in Redstone, the honey bottle in Foodstuffs, the bee's spawn egg among the others); the mod has no tab of its own.

*   **Bees**: pollinating flowers, growing crops, making honey, going home at night and in rain, anger shared with their hive mates, stinging and dying after a sting, breeding and following you for flowers. 1.15's model, textures, sounds and particles, and a spawn egg.
*   **Bee nests** on oak and birch trees in plains, sunflower plains, flower forests, forests and birch forests, and on oak and birch trees grown from saplings near flowers. **Beehives** to build.
*   **Honey**: shears and glass bottles on full nests and hives, by hand or from a dispenser; campfires and fire underneath keep bees calm; Silk Touch moves nests and hives with their bees; comparators read honey.
*   **Honey bottle**, **honeycomb**, **honey block** (slow, sticky, slides, breaks falls, moves with pistons but not with slime) and **honeycomb block**.
*   **Iron golems** crack as they are hurt and are repaired with iron ingots.
*   **1.15's smaller changes**, each with its own switch: end stone bricks, boats as fuel, sponges drying in the Nether, rails facing you, fern seeds, baby zombies from spawn eggs, baby animals, parrots, eating in Creative, beds setting your spawn, five new game rules, dispensers (bottles, armor stands, campfires, fireworks), bells on redstone, splash texts, shift-clicking into the crafting grid, elytra opening while rising, toggle sneak and sprint, the Two by Two and A Balanced Diet advancements, parrots staying on while riding, experience dropping with the loot.
*   **1.15's look and feel**, through Backport Core: the bee panorama on the title screen, 1.15's three new splashes ("Buzzy Bees!" among them), **Sneak** and **Sprint** as Hold or Toggle on Accessibility Settings (the same setting as `tweaks.toggleSneak` and `tweaks.toggleSprint`), a hardcore death screen whose second button is **Title screen**, and a window title that says what you are playing ("Minecraft* 1.12.2 - Singleplayer"). When a newer update backport is installed too, the newest update's look wins, as in vanilla.
*   Advancements *Sticky Situation*, *Bee Our Guest* and *Total Beelocation*.
*   **Seed maps: worlds match 1.15.** A new world generated with this mod (and the 1.13 and 1.14 backports) matches what 1.15 generates for the same seed, so seed map sites set to 1.15 show its biomes and structures where they really are. Checked against Minecraft 1.15.2's own code over six seeds. The biome of each block blends between biomes as in 1.15, so villages, temples and other structures near a biome's edge start where 1.15 starts them. With a backport of a later update installed as well, the world matches that update instead.
*   Works with Village & Pillage Backport's campfires, flowers, berry bushes, bells and golems, and brings its own campfire when Village & Pillage isn't installed.
*   Worlds that had another mod's bees, nests, hives and honey keep them, converted to this mod's, with the bees and honey inside.
*   Eighteen languages.
