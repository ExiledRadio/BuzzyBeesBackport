# Buzzy Bees Backport

All of 1.15 Buzzy Bees, on Minecraft 1.12.2. It needs Backport Core on the client and the server.

> 💬 [Join the Discord](https://discord.gg/kxQvMDJBTN) for bug reports and questions.

![Bees around their beehives in a flower field](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2180/907/bb-beehives-flower-field-jpg.jpg)

## Bees

Bees fly between flowers and their nest, gather nectar and make honey. Pollinated bees grow the crops they fly over. They head home at night and when it rains.

Bees are neutral. Hit one and its hive mates come after you too. A sting poisons you, and the bee dies a minute later.

Any flower breeds them, and they follow you while you hold one.

![Angry bees swarming a zombie](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2180/906/bb-angry-bees-zombie-jpg.jpg)

## Nests and hives

Bee nests grow on oak and birch trees in plains, flower forests and forests. An oak or birch sapling that grows near a flower sometimes gets one too.

Build beehives from planks and honeycomb. When a nest or hive is full, shears give honeycomb and a glass bottle gives honey. A campfire underneath keeps the bees calm. Silk Touch moves a nest or hive with its bees inside.

![A bee nest on an oak tree](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2180/908/bb-bee-nest-oak-tree-jpg.jpg)

## Honey

The honey bottle feeds you and cures poison. The honey block slows you down, lets you slide down its sides and breaks your fall. Pistons move it like slime, but honey and slime don't stick to each other.

![Honey and honeycomb blocks at sunset](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2180/905/bb-honeycomb-hive-sunset-jpg.jpg)

## Iron golems

Iron golems crack as they get hurt. Iron ingots repair them.

![Cracked iron golems at the village bell](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2180/909/bb-cracked-iron-golems-jpg.jpg)

## The rest of 1.15

You also get end stone bricks as hard as stone, boats that burn as fuel, sponges that dry in the Nether, rails that face you, eating in Creative, beds that set your spawn, the new game rules, toggle sneak and sprint, and more.

Every feature can be turned off in Backport Core's settings screen, under Mod Options.

## Compatibility

**Dynamic Trees**: its oaks and birches get bee nests, with 1.15's chances.

**[Village & Pillage Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/villager-backport)**: its campfires calm bees, bees pollinate its cornflowers, lilies of the valley and wither roses and grow its berry bushes, its bell rings from redstone, and its village golems crack like any other. Some foxes spawn as babies. Without it, this mod brings its own campfire.

**[Update Aquatic Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/update-aquatic-backport)**: with `doInsomnia` off, phantoms stop spawning. Drowned spawn eggs make baby drowned.

**[Caves & Cliffs Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/caves-cliffs-backport)**: honeycomb waxes copper and makes candles, goats can't jump off honey blocks, and trees in its 1.18 worlds get bee nests.

**[Nether Update Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/nether-backport)**: angry bees forgive a player who dies, with its `forgiveDeadPlayers` setting.

**[Wild Update Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/wild-update-backport)**: some mangrove trees grow bee nests.

**[Trails & Tales Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/trails-tales-backport)**: honeycomb waxes signs and hanging signs.

**[Tricky Trials Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/trials-backport)**: honeycomb waxes its copper.

**[Game Drops Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/game-drops-backport)**: its sulfur cubes swallow honeycomb blocks and turn sticky, and its camp trees get bee nests.

## Requirements

Minecraft 1.12.2 with Forge 14.23.5.2847 or newer, and [Backport Core](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/backport-core) 1.4.7 or newer. The CurseForge app installs Backport Core and Fluidlogged API with it.
