# Stream G: nest generation

Package `nestgen`, API `api/BeeNests`. Config `nests` (`enabled`, `moddedBiomes`). No entities or packets.
Needs stream B's `bee_nest` (by name; does nothing without it). Built in the first session.

## Changelog section

*   **Bee nests** grow on oak and birch trees in newly generated plains and sunflower plains (1 tree in 20),
    flower forests (1 in 50), and forests and birch forests (1 in 500), with 2 or 3 bees inside.
*   An oak or birch sapling that grows with a flower within two blocks gets a nest 1 time in 20, anywhere.
*   Other mods' plains and broadleaf forests get nests too (`nests.moddedBiomes`).

## How it works

*   1.12.2 has no hook per tree, so after a chunk is populated `NestGenerator` looks at its 16x16 decoration
    area: down each column through air and leaves to an oak or birch log, down the trunk to dirt or grass.
    Each trunk found gets its biome's chance (`BeeNests.chance`; 1.15.2's table, Forge's biome dictionary
    for other mods' biomes, `BeeNests.setBiomeChance` for later backports).
*   Placement as 1.15's `BeehiveTreeDecorator`: one block under the lowest leaves (not under the trunk's
    base), on the west, east or south side, facing south, only where the nest and the block south of it are
    air; 2-3 bees, each 0-598 ticks into its rest.
*   Saplings: `SaplingGrowTreeEvent` (Forge's terrain bus) fires before the tree exists, so a qualifying
    oak or birch sapling is remembered, with the 5% already rolled, and the nest goes on at the end of the
    tick if a tree grew. Flowers: 1.15.2's box, 2 across and 1 up or down, any of `BeeFlowers`.
*   The per-chunk random is seeded from the world seed and chunk, so a seed gives the same nests.

## Tested headlessly

`./gradlew runServer -Pselftest=scaffold,honey,bees,nests` on port 25803: **`SELFTEST DONE pass=195
fail=0`** (nests 33), `check_assets` 0 errors.

*   The chance table for 1.12.2's biomes, none in desert, taiga or dark forest; oak and birch count, spruce
    does not.
*   A hand-built oak: one trunk, one nest, one block below the lowest leaves, beside the trunk on its west,
    east or south side, facing south, air in front, 2-3 bees. A spruce gets none.
*   A birch grown from a sapling with a dandelion near gets a nest; one with no flower does not.
*   Real terrain: a forest found by the biome provider far from spawn, 49 chunks generated by the world
    with every tree made eligible: at least 5 nests in the middle 16 chunks, every one against a trunk
    and holding bees.

## Not tested

*   The real rates (5%, 2%, 0.2%) over a large pregenerated area, and nests in other mods' biomes

    way only if they use vanilla oak and birch logs.
